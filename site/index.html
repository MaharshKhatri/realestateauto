Sell your New Jersey house as-is

We're local real estate investors buying houses in Middlesex, Union, Essex, Hudson, Passaic, Bergen, Camden, Mercer and Monmouth counties. No repairs, no cleanout, no showings, no agent commissions — you pick the closing date.

  • No agent commissionYou pay only your normal seller costs (your attorney and New Jersey's realty transfer fee) unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • No repairs, no cleanoutTake what you want and leave the rest. We buy the house in its current condition.
  • Hard situations are fineBuried oil tank, code violations, tenants in place, an estate in probate, a house that's been vacant for years — tell us, and we'll factor it into a written offer.
  • Your attorney reviews everythingNew Jersey contracts include a 3-business-day attorney review. If it's your home and it's in foreclosure, you also get a 10-business-day right to cancel.

Questions people ask

Will you pay full retail price?
No — an investor's as-is offer is usually below what a fully repaired house would sell for on the market. If your house is in good shape and you have time, listing it with an agent may net you more; we'll tell you honestly if we think so.

My house is in foreclosure. Can you help?
We can make an offer to buy it. We can't stop, postpone or negotiate your foreclosure, and we're not a substitute for legal advice — call a HUD-approved counselor (1-800-569-4287) or Legal Services of New Jersey (1-888-576-5529), both free. If it's your home, New Jersey's Foreclosure Rescue Fraud Prevention Act protects you: an attorney-drafted contract, at least 82% of fair market value, and 10 business days to cancel.

Do you buy with tenants in place?
Yes. Leases and security deposits transfer at closing under New Jersey law.

Are you agents?
No. We're investors buying for ourselves (or assigning our contract to another investor). We don't represent you, and you should have your own attorney.
